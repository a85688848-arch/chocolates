Our Heritage & Passion

Handmade Confections With Devotion to Detail

“Chocolate is not merely a confection — it is an emotional language of celebration, gratitude, and devotion.”

Founded on the uncompromising pursuit of French and Swiss confectionery heritage, Choclétier Noir began as an artisanal atelier dedicated to the art of bespoke chocolate gifting. We source our cacao exclusively from ethically managed single-estate plantations in Ecuador and Madagascar, ensuring rich, nuanced flavor profiles that honor the earth.

Every ganache is slow-simmered in small batches with fresh dairy cream, genuine Tahitian vanilla beans, and roasted Mediterranean nuts. We never use artificial preservatives, hydrogenated oils, or palm fat. When you gift Choclétier Noir, you give more than chocolate: you give an unforgettable masterpiece.
